Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GTA6 Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Kishor Krishna T S, to upload his own gameplay clips to his own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: it reads video files in one folder chosen by the owner, and it creates and updates one small file in that folder that records which clips have been posted.
  • YouTube: it uploads videos to the owner's channel.

What it does with that data

Clips are passed straight from Google Drive to YouTube. They are not stored, sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or any other purpose. Sign-in tokens are kept only in the owner's own server settings.

Google API data

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
